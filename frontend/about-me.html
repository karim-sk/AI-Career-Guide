About AI Career Navigator

AI Career Navigator is an AI-powered career guidance platform designed to help students assess their technical skills, identify skill gaps, explore suitable career paths, and receive personalized learning roadmaps. The platform combines skill assessment, artificial intelligence, career analysis, and personalized learning guidance in one place.
